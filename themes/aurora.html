When a trader at a bank takes a call on Microsoft Teams, the law says that call must be recorded and stored safely. If a recording silently fails, the bank has a real problem. My job is to run the cloud platform that does the recording, so every call is captured, upgrades happen without drama, and if something breaks at 3 a.m., it's caught and fixed before anyone notices.

I'm a DevOps Engineer with 3+ years of building CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, containers and GitOps across Microsoft Azure, Amazon AWS and Google Cloud.

At NICE I turned a manual, spreadsheet-driven upgrade process into self-running automation pipelines and stopped false alarms from waking people up. At Amagi I launched TV streaming channels at 99.99% uptime, and at XchangeTechs I ran weekly releases with under two minutes of downtime.

I also build small AI-assisted tools (with Claude) that speed up log analysis and alert triage.
